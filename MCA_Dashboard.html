There was no MCA testing in Rochester Public School District in 2023 due to a cyberattack. In 2026, the MCA-IV Reading Test was introduced, and in 2025, the MCA-IV Science Test was introduced. Be cautious with comparisons to previous years. In change mode, each value is the district's percent proficient minus its 2019 value, in percentage points, so the 2019 column is 0. In the bar chart, the change is measured from 2019 to the selected year, so choose 2026 for the full 2019 to 2026 change. In rank mode, 1 is the highest percent proficient among the districts currently selected for that grade and year, so ranks change when you change the selection. Tied districts share a rank. Places gained is the change in rank from 2019 to 2026, so a positive number means the district moved up. A district can be in more than one group (for example, Edina is both Top 30 and Similar).
