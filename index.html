MCA Proficiency Dashboard

Interactive views of Minnesota Comprehensive Assessment results for 36 school districts, 2019 through 2026. Use the dashboard to explore reading, math, and science.

Open the dashboard

Choose reading, math, or science. Compare districts by grade, year, and display type: percent proficient, rank, or change from 2019.

How to use the dashboard

  • Pick a subject and grade, then check the districts you want to compare. Eastern Carver County is always shown.
  • Use the Display menu to switch between percent proficient, rank among the selected districts, and change from 2019.
  • Use the trend chart to follow results over time, and the comparison chart to compare districts in one year.

Notes

  • There was no MCA testing in 2020.
  • Years are spring test years. For example, 2019 is the 2018-19 school year.
  • The 2026 results come from the new MCA/ALTMCA test, so compare them with earlier years carefully.
  • There was no MCA testing in Rochester Public School District in 2023 due to a cyberattack.

Source: Minnesota Department of Education, MCA/MTAS/ALTMCA public accountability data (District tab), 2019 through 2026. Compiled October 21, 2026.
