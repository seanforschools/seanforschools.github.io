Open Enrollment: Net In vs Out by District

Students entering (IN) and leaving (OUT) each district under open enrollment, by school year, 2016-17 through 2025-26. Net = IN minus OUT. Positive values mean more students came in than went out.

Net by district and year

Districts are ordered by the absolute value of net in 2025-26, largest to smallest. Click a district name to open its detail view below.

Ranked from most net IN to most net OUT. Districts with no flows in the selected year are not shown.

Notes

  • Years are school years. For example, 2025-26 is the 2025-26 school year.
  • A blank IN or OUT entry in the source file is counted as zero.
  • District names were standardized so the same district appears once. For example, "Saint Paul" and "St. Paul" are combined, and "Burnsville" and "Burnsville-Eagan-Savage" are combined because the district was renamed and the two names appear in different years.
  • Values are counts of students as reported in the source file. Small changes between years can reflect enrollment policy and reporting differences as well as real shifts.
